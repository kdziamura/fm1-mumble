# What is kept over power-off: the line, a character's sound and mute, the
# loop's notes and size, the scale. Saved 3 s after the last change (not while
# a key is held or recording); a reboot brings it all back.
wait 600
tapkey 7
tapkey 4
tapkey 24
expect length 3
# SING: K1 moves HERO's PITCH; SCL C major; REC two notes into the loop.
tap GLO
expect mode 1
turn K1 6
wait 50
expect edited 1
tap SEL
expect scale 1
tap REC
expect recording 1
wait 300
tapkey 0
wait 400
tapkey 4
wait 100
tap REC
expect recording 0
expect takes 2
tap PLAY
wait 100
expect song 0
# LOOP: HERO muted, the loop 4 bars at 100 BPM.
tap GLO
expect mode 2
turn K1 1
turn K3 1
turn K4 -20
wait 50
expect mute 1
expect bpm <120
# Left alone 3 s: saved. A reboot: RAM gone, the flash read back.
wait 3500
expect store_writes >0
reboot
wait 600
expect mode 0
expect length 3
expect edited 1
expect scale 1
expect takes 2
expect mute 1
expect bpm <120
